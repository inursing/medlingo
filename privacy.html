MedLingo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What MedLingo is

MedLingo is a medical-English vocabulary review site for students in an EMI course, run by the course teacher. Using it without signing in stores your progress only in your own browser.

What we collect when you sign in with Google

  • Your Google account email address and display name (from Google Sign-In).
  • The student ID and name you enter once to link your account to the class list.
  • Learning activity: visits, cards viewed per unit, quiz attempts and scores, and the words you mark as difficult.

We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How it is used and stored

The data is saved in a Google Sheet owned by the course teacher and is used only to sync your progress across devices and to let the teacher follow class progress. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Your choices

You can use MedLingo without signing in. To have your record corrected or deleted, contact the teacher at a103262003@gmail.com.

中文摘要

使用 Google 登入時，本網站會記錄你的 Google Email、名稱、你填寫的學號與姓名，以及學習紀錄（造訪次數、看過的卡片、測驗成績、標記的不熟單字），存放在授課老師的 Google 試算表，僅用於跨裝置同步與課程學習追蹤，不會提供給第三方。不登入也可以使用，紀錄只存在你的瀏覽器。如需更正或刪除紀錄，請聯絡授課老師。
